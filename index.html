What it is and why it matters

Synonyms: ZHS, zinc hexahydroxystannate, zinc tin hydroxide, Flamtard H (William Blythe), Storflam ZHS (Joseph Storey), Alcanex ZHS, Charmax LS ZHS, ZnSn(OH)6 [2][5][13][24].

ZHS is a white, water-insoluble inorganic powder that works in two phases at once: it boosts gas-phase flame inhibition by halogenated systems and promotes char in the condensed phase, cutting smoke [9][57]. It was developed as a non-toxic alternative to antimony trioxide (ATO, EU Carc. 2) [21][41]. Above ~220 °C it releases ≈15 wt % water, so it suits PVC and other polymers processed below that temperature; the anhydrous sister product, zinc stannate (ZnSnO3, CAS 12036-37-2), is used for higher-temperature resins [9][10]. Typical loading is only 2–7 % [13], and in rigid PVC ~3 phr already gives strong fire and smoke benefits [21].

Industry Applications, End-Products & Market Sizes

A. Wire & cable — PVC FRLS, LSZH/HFFR, CR/CSM jackets (largest)

ZHS was first developed for halogen-containing flexible PVC for wires and cables; it raises oxygen index while lowering smoke, and Indian suppliers position it as a replacement for molybdenum trioxide in FRLS compounds [14][15]. A patented synergy lets formulators cut ATO to ≤1.5 wt % when combined with zinc (hydroxy)stannate [22].

B. Rigid & flexible PVC (profiles, sheets, flooring, coated fabrics)

In rigid PVC ZHS at ~3 phr outperformed zinc borate on fire and smoke in cone-calorimeter and LOI tests [21]. Trade evidence shows ZHS shipped to an Indonesian fabric mill (PT Ateja Tritunggal, 1.05 t in 2026) and a Vietnamese fire-curtain maker [TD].

C. Rubber, polyester/thermosets & FR paints

Flamtard H is sold as a synergist for FR paints, unsaturated polyester, polyamides, PBT and elastomers, and as a co-additive with phosphate FRs to strengthen char [8][10]. Rubber buyers in the extract: PT Rubber Pan Java (ID), LLC Roka-Cell (UZ — insulating materials), Mega Vietnam [TD].

D. Friction materials & electronics

ZHS is also a solid-lubricant additive in friction materials [8]; zinc stannate is increasingly used in “green” electronics (epoxy PCBs) [9][10]. Nano zinc stannate is researched for batteries/sensors (non-FR, R&D only).

Strategic Business Frameworks

9A — SWOT (our company entering ZHS)

Strengths

  • Non-hazardous, ATO-free story fits India's FRLS/LSZH upgrade
  • Simple logistics: non-DG powder, 25 kg bags
  • Existing China sourcing network and India sales reach to cable compounders
  • Can bundle with ATO, zinc borate, ATH, molybdates as a smoke-suppression basket

Weaknesses

  • No tin base in India; price fully tin-indexed

Feasibility Summary & Management Recommendation

VERDICT: CONDITIONAL
Enter ZHS as an asset-light trading and sourcing line inside a smoke-suppressant / ATO-alternative basket. Do not build capacity until India offtake reaches ≥200 t/yr with tin-indexed pricing.

Justification

Demand drivers are real — China's antimony export controls [37], ATO's carcinogen classification [41] and India's FR/FRLS/LSZH cable upgrade [51][52]. But ZHS is a low-volume synergist whose price is ≈60 % tin, supply is ≈70–80 % Chinese, and China's ATO price has fallen sharply in 2026 [38], so the case rests on smoke/toxicity performance rather than cost. The user's trade extract confirms an active but thin merchant market: 18 clean valued lots worth only US$62k in 31 months, with bulk 500 kg lots at US$34–35/kg [TD].
